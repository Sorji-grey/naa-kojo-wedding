Questions

Kindly answered.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation says how many seats we've saved for you, and the RSVP form below matches it. If you think we've missed someone, message us and we'll do our best.

Are children welcome?

Children are very welcome at Friday's traditional. Saturday's reception is adults only, so parents can enjoy a night off.

What should I wear?

Friday: kente or African print, ideally in midnight blue and gold. Saturday: black tie or formal, with a touch of gold if you like. Comfortable shoes for dancing are strongly encouraged.

Can I take photos during the service?

We'd love an unplugged ceremony: phones away in church, please. Our photographer will share everything after. At the reception, snap away and tag #NaaAndKojo27.

I'm travelling from abroad. Any tips?

Fly into Kotoka International (ACC) and check whether you need a visa early. April is warm and humid, around 31°C, so pack light fabrics. Bolt and Uber work well across Accra.

When do you need my RSVP?

By 1 March 2027, so we can give final numbers to the caterers. You can change your answer any time before then.
